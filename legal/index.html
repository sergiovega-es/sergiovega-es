2. What I do

I read electricity bills for households in Spain, explain them in plain language and, when a change makes sense, help the client switch tariff or supplier. The bill check is free for the person who asks for it. If a client contracts a new supply through me, that supplier pays me; the client never does. If the bill is fine, I say so and nothing changes.

I do not act on behalf of any electricity supplier and I am not a party to the contract between you and your supplier. The figures I give are calculated from the bill you send me and the prices published by the suppliers on the date of the check; they are an estimate for your decision, not a quotation that binds the supplier.

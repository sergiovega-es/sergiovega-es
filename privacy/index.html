3. Who else sees your data

I do not sell data and I do not pass it to anyone for their own marketing. The companies that process data on my behalf, under a data-processing agreement, are:

  • MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, Vilnius, Lithuania, EU): stores the email list and sends the guide and my emails.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.: Facebook and Instagram ads, lead forms and the Meta pixel on my website. WhatsApp messages travel through WhatsApp (also Meta), end-to-end encrypted.
  • Vercel Inc. (USA) and GitHub Inc. (USA): hosting of the website.
  • Google LLC: fonts loaded on the website and my email service.

If you ask me to switch your supply, your details go to the electricity supplier you have chosen, who then becomes responsible for them under its own privacy policy. Nothing goes to any supplier before you say so.

Some of these providers are in the United States. Transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

4. How long I keep it

  • Your email: until you unsubscribe. After that I keep only the minimum needed to make sure I do not email you again.
  • Bills and messages: while I am dealing with your request and, once it is closed, for as long as Spanish law can require me to be able to prove what was done (in general, up to five years), unless you ask me to delete them earlier and no legal duty prevents it.
  • Advertising data: according to Meta's own retention periods (currently up to 180 days for pixel data).

5. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to see the data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how I use it, receive it in a portable format, or object to a use based on my legitimate interest. You can also withdraw consent whenever you like; that does not affect what was done before. Write to sergio@sergiovega.es or to the postal address above. I answer within a month.

If you think I have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, www.aepd.es).

6. Other points

  • My services are for adults. I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
  • I keep your data on systems protected with passwords and two-factor authentication, and I share bills only with the people strictly needed to deal with your request.
  • If I change this policy, the date at the top changes too.

WHAT'S IN THE GUIDE

Five lines. Which ones matter in your business.

01

The access tariff

2.0TD, 3.0TD or 6.1TD. Set by how your business is connected, not by your supplier, and easy to get wrong. If there's a transformer on your property, has anyone checked that your tariff matches it?

02

Contracted power and the maxímetro

Paid every day of the year, open or closed. A small factory pays for 173 kW and has never used more than 70. And when the maxímetro shows 0, your meter isn't being read and a correction can arrive months later.

03

Reactive energy

On 3.0TD and 6.1TD bills, fridges, compressors, pumps and air conditioning can cost you money for energy that buys nothing. On one small hotel's bills, between 6 and 14 euros every single month.

04

Meter rental

Regulated, and on 3.0TD and 6.1TD often around a euro a day, and it rarely changes when you switch. That's exactly why saving studies get it wrong: about 430 euros a year of one hotel's promised saving was rent.

05

Fixed or indexed, and your contract dates

In one small hotel's cheapest period, the energy price doubled between April and September. Same contract, same supplier, and nobody called them. Plus the renewal and early-exit dates most owners don't know.

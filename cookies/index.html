Cookies

Last updated: 3 October 2026

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser. My websites use very few, and none of them is needed to read the pages or to download the guide.

Which cookies and why

CookieSet byWhat forLasts
_fbp, _fbcMeta (Facebook / Instagram) pixelTells Meta that someone who saw one of my ads visited the page or filled in the form, so I can measure whether the ads work and show them to similar people. Also used by Meta for its own purposes as described in its privacy policy.Up to 90 days
Form cookies (ml_…)MailerLiteRemember that you have already filled in the guide form, so it does not show again.Up to 1 year

I do not use Google Analytics or any other analytics tool.

How to switch them off

  • In your browser: block or delete cookies for sergiovega.eu in the privacy settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge all allow it per site). The pages work the same without them.
  • In Meta: you can limit how Facebook and Instagram use data from other websites in your Off-Facebook activity settings and in your ad preferences.

More

How personal data is handled is explained in the privacy policy.

Una cookie es un pequeño archivo que una web guarda en tu navegador. Mis webs usan muy pocas y ninguna hace falta para leer las páginas ni para descargar la guía.

Qué cookies y para qué

CookieLa ponePara quéDura

Cómo desactivarlas

  • En el navegador: bloquea o borra las cookies de sergiovega.eu en los ajustes de privacidad (Chrome, Safari, Firefox y Edge lo permiten por sitio). Las páginas funcionan igual sin ellas.
  • En Meta: puedes limitar el uso que Facebook e Instagram hacen de los datos de otras webs en los ajustes de actividad fuera de Facebook y en tus preferencias de anuncios.
